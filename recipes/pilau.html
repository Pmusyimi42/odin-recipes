Pilau Recipe

Description

Pilau is a popular East African rice dish, especially loved in Kenya and Tanzania. It is prepared by cooking rice with aromatic spices such as cumin, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and black pepper, which give it a rich fragrance and distinctive taste. The rice is usually cooked with beef, chicken, or goat meat, along with onions, garlic, ginger, and sometimes tomatoes. The combination of spices and meat broth gives pilau its characteristic brown colour and delicious flavour.

Pilau is commonly served during celebrations, weddings, family gatherings, and special occasions, although many people also enjoy it as an everyday meal. In Kenya, it is often accompanied by kachumbari, a fresh salad made with tomatoes, onions, coriander, and lemon juice. The dish is appreciated for its satisfying taste, appealing aroma, and cultural significance, bringing people together to share a delicious and memorable meal.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of rice
  • 500g beef, cut into small pieces
  • 2-3 large onions
  • 3-4 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 2-3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1-2 potatoes (optional)
  • 4-5 cups beef broth or water
  • salt to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 30 minutes.
  2. In a large pot, heat the cooking oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown.
  3. Add the crushed garlic and grated ginger to the pot, and sauté for another minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the beef pieces to the pot and cook until they are browned on all sides.
  5. If using potatoes, peel and cut them into cubes, then add them to the pot and cook for a few minutes.
  6. Add the soaked rice to the pot and stir well to combine with the meat and onions.
  7. Pour in the beef broth or water, ensuring that it covers the rice by about an inch. Season with salt to taste.
  8. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let it simmer for about 20-25 minutes or until the rice is cooked and has absorbed all the liquid.
  9. Once cooked, fluff the rice gently with a fork and serve hot with kachumbari or your preferred side dish.
