Matoke Recipe

Description

This is a delicious Kenyan home-style meal with soft green bananas, tender beef and a rich tomato-onion gravy. Serve it for lunch or supper with avocado or kachumbari.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef, cut into cubes
  • 1 kg green bananas (matoke), peeled and sliced
  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • 3-4 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2-3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 4-5 cups beef broth or water
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pot, heat the cooking oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown.
  2. Add the minced garlic and sauté for another minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the beef cubes to the pot and cook until they are browned on all sides.
  4. Add the tomato paste and stir well to coat the beef and onions. Cook for a few minutes until the tomato paste is well incorporated.
  5. Pour in the beef broth or water, ensuring that it covers the beef. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let it simmer for about 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until the beef is tender.
  6. Add the sliced green bananas (matoke) to the pot and stir gently to combine with the beef and gravy. Cover the pot and let it simmer for an additional 20-25 minutes, or until the bananas are soft and cooked through.
  7. Once the matoke is cooked, taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary. Serve hot with avocado or kachumbari on the side.
