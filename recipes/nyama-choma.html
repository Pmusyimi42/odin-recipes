Nyama Choma

Kenyan nyama choma is meat, usually goat or beef, grilled until tender and browned. It is commonly served with kachumbari and ugali, making it a favourite for family gatherings and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg meat (goat or beef)
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced

Instructions

  1. Marinate the meat with salt, black pepper, garlic, and ginger for at least 2 hours.
  2. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  3. Grill the meat for about 15-20 minutes on each side or until fully cooked and browned.
  4. Serve hot with kachumbari and ugali.
